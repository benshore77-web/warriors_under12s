What is the Bleep Test?

The Multi-Stage Fitness Test (MSFT), also known as the bleep test, is a progressive cardiovascular endurance test that measures aerobic capacity (VO2 max).

Met Police 15m Version

The test we use is the 15-metre version employed by the Metropolitan Police for fitness assessments:

  • Distance: Players run back and forth between two lines 15 metres apart
  • Progression: The test starts at a slow pace and gradually increases in speed
  • Levels & Shuttles: Each level consists of a set number of shuttles (runs between the lines)
  • Audio Cues: A beep signals when to reach the next line - you must reach it before the beep
  • Completion: The test continues until you can no longer keep pace with the beeps

Why is it Useful?

  • Measures Cardiovascular Fitness: Tests your heart and lungs' ability to supply oxygen during intense exercise
  • Rugby-Specific: Mimics the stop-start nature of rugby with short sprints and quick turns
  • Tracks Progress: Monthly testing shows improvement in fitness levels over time
  • Motivation: Provides clear, measurable goals to work towards
  • Team Building: Encourages players to push themselves alongside teammates
